Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This policy explains how Sofi Studios ("we") handles information when we sign in to Google to publish videos on our YouTube channels.

Information we access

We request the Google permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload. We use it only to upload videos to YouTube channels owned by Sofi Studios and to set their title, description, tags and visibility. We do not read, collect or store any other Google account data.

How it is stored

The sign-in credentials Google issues are kept on our private systems, used only for publishing to our own channels, and never sold or shared with third parties.

Google API Services

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Publishing is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Visitors to this website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored credentials are deleted on request.
